Calendar days vs working days

The default mode adds or subtracts calendar days — every day counts, including weekends and leap days, and the calculator uses real month lengths rather than a flat 30-day approximation. Tick the working days option and the count skips Saturdays and Sundays entirely, which is what contracts mean by "within 10 business days".

Enter a negative number to move backwards: a payment due 15 days before an event, a notice period that runs backwards from a departure date, or a cooling-off window counted back from a signature.

Where this comes up in practice

  • Contract deadlines: "net 30" invoices, notice periods, and delivery windows expressed in business days.
  • Planning: medication cycles, subscription renewals, project milestones, travel with a fixed return.
  • Money dates: interest accrual windows and payment due dates feeding into the loan calculator.

Public holidays are not excluded in working-days mode — national holiday calendars vary, so subtract those manually if your deadline depends on them. Dates are interpreted in your device's local time zone.
