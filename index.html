Why these calculators exist

Most calculator sites bury the answer under pop-ups and auto-playing ads. Numora is built the other way around: you type your numbers, the result updates instantly, and nothing you enter leaves your device. The pages are small enough to load on a bad connection, and every formula is documented on the page itself so you can check the math.

Each tool is accurate enough for everyday decisions — comparing loan offers, checking a unit conversion while travelling, splitting a dinner bill. For medical, legal or investment decisions, treat these as a first estimate and confirm with a professional.

Frequently asked questions

Are Numora calculators free?

Yes. Every calculator is free to use, with no sign-up and no limits.

Do you store my numbers?

No. All calculations run in your browser. The values you type are never sent to a server.

How accurate are the results?

Results use standard, published formulas and full double-precision arithmetic, rounding only for display. Financial and health results are estimates, not professional advice.

Can I use Numora on my phone?

Yes. The site is fully responsive and works in any modern mobile browser.
