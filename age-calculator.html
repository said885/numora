How exact age is counted

The calculator uses the same method as passports and identity documents: count the complete years between the two dates, then the complete months since the last birthday anniversary, then the remaining days. Leap days are handled naturally because the calculation is calendar-based, not a simple division of one date by another.

The "total days lived" figure is the plain difference between the two dates at 86,400 seconds per day. The "next birthday" figure counts to the upcoming anniversary — useful for planning, and occasionally for countdowns.

Things this age is useful for

  • Legal thresholds: driving licences, voting, retirement, age-restricted products — all key off the birthday anniversary.
  • Insurance and finance: many quotes depend on your exact age in whole years, not your birth year.
  • Health milestones: screening programmes (mammograms, colonoscopies) start at specific ages.
  • Just for fun: total days lived, or how many days to the next birthday.

Dates are interpreted in your device's local time zone. For legal purposes, always confirm with the relevant authority's own rule.
