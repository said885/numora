Conversions people look up most

  • Kilometers to miles: 1 km = 0.621371 mi · 1 mi = 1.609344 km
  • Kilograms to pounds: 1 kg = 2.20462 lb · 1 lb = 0.453592 kg
  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
  • Centimeters to inches: 1 cm = 0.393701 in · 1 in = 2.54 cm
  • Stone to kilograms: 1 st = 6.35029 kg

Why exact factors matter

Length and weight conversions here use the exact international definitions (the inch is exactly 2.54 cm, the pound exactly 0.45359237 kg). Many sites round these to two or three decimals, which quietly introduces errors of a gram or a millimetre per conversion — noticeable when you chain several conversions together, for example in recipes, 3D printing or engineering specs.

Temperature is different: it is an interval scale with an offset, so the converter applies the full formula rather than a multiplication factor. Kelvin starts at absolute zero, which is why 0 °C = 32 °F but 0 K = −273.15 °C.
