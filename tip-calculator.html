Quick tipping reference

  • Standard service (US): 15–20% of the pre-tax bill.
  • Excellent service: 20–25%, or round up generously.
  • Counter service / takeout: 0–10% is perfectly acceptable.
  • Europe: service is usually included; leaving 5–10% for exceptional service is a nice gesture, not an obligation.
  • Japan, South Korea, Australia: tipping is not customary and can even cause confusion.

Fair splitting, without the argument

Enter the total bill, pick a tip percentage and set the number of people — the calculator shows what each person owes, tip included. If someone ordered significantly more, do it in two steps: total the expensive items first, tip on the remainder for the rest of the table, then add the difference per person.

Tipping customs vary widely by country and context. When in doubt, follow local norms — the calculator will handle the arithmetic either way.
